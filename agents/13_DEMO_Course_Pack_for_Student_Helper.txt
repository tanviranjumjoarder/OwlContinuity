=== 5. RUBRIC: DECISION MEMO (100 points) ===

- Evidence (35 points): uses data from the lab's evidence table to support the recommendation.
- Comparison (25 points): explains why the other option was rejected.
- Writing and format (15 points): professional memo format, maximum 600 words, one PDF named Lastname_Memo.pdf.
