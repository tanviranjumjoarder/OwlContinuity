=== 2. COURSE DESCRIPTION AND LEARNING OUTCOMES ===
This course teaches students basic concepts of Big Data Analytics with focus on social network analysis and modeling. The class covers three major topics: graphs and social network models, Big Data Analytics platform and MapReduce (Hadoop) programming, and social network analytics and mining algorithms.
Students will: (1) define key concepts in graph theory (nodes, edges, directed/undirected graphs) and calculate essential graph metrics (centrality, clustering coefficient, degree distribution) for social networks; (2) build, analyze, and visualize social networks using Python libraries (e.g., NetworkX, Matplotlib); (3) understand the role of big data analytics in social network analysis, including the use of distributed frameworks (e.g., Hadoop, Spark) and algorithms (e.g., PageRank, community detection) for analyzing large-scale networks; (4) practically use big data analytics framework to analyze social networks and effectively interpret the results.

=== 4. GRADING ===
Project (4 parts): 60% (15% each part). Quizzes: 40%. Total 100%.
Quizzes: there will be four quizzes, consisting of both theoretical and mathematical questions. Quizzes will be completed in person.
Project: instead of a final exam, students will complete a project consisting of 4 parts in which they apply big data analytics concepts to perform a social network analysis.
Grading scale: A 100-94%, A- <94-90%, B+ <90-87%, B <87-83%, B- <83-80%, C+ <80-77%, C <77-73%, C- <73-70%, D+ <70-67%, D <67-63%, D- <63-60%, F <60-0%.

=== 5. MAKE-UP TESTS, LATE WORK AND INCOMPLETES ===
Quizzes must be taken on the dates specified in class, no exceptions. In the last class of the course, students will have the opportunity to make up a SINGLE quiz they missed during the semester.
Strictly adhering to homework assignment deadlines is strongly encouraged. Late work submitted within 24 hours of the deadline receives 10% grade penalty. Late work received within 48 hours of the deadline receives 20% grade penalty. No late work is accepted 48 hours after the deadline.
Grades of Incomplete ("I") are reserved for students who are passing a course but have not completed all the required work because of exceptional circumstances. If you have solid evidence of a medical or otherwise serious emergency, please contact the instructor as soon as possible to discuss the possibility of an Incomplete grade.

=== 6. RECORDING OF LECTURES ===
Students may record video or audio of class lectures for their own personal educational use. Recording class activities other than class lectures, including student presentations (individual or group), class discussion (except when incidental to a lecture), labs, academic exercises involving student participation, test or examination administrations, field trips and private conversations, is prohibited. Recordings may not be used as a substitute for class participation or attendance and may not be published or shared without the written consent of the faculty member.

=== 7. COURSE AI POLICY ===
